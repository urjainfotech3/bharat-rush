1. Who we are

Bharat Rush: City Tracks ("the game") is made by Urja Infotech, Kachchh, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). You can contact us at urjainfotech3@gmail.com.

2. Information saved on your device

The game saves the following on your phone so you can continue where you left off: best distance, coins, items you have bought or unlocked, upgrades, mission progress, sound and graphics settings, and whether you have finished the tutorial. This information stays on your device. We do not send it to our own servers. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone audio. The game does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not offer chat or contact with other players.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game may show ads, including optional "watch an ad for a reward" videos and occasional full-screen ads between runs. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location (for example, your city);
  • device and app information such as phone model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interaction data such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic information about how the ad SDK performs.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings (usually Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings.

5. In-app purchases

The game may offer optional purchases such as coin packs, outfits or removing ads. All payments are handled by Google Play. We receive only the information needed to deliver your purchase (for example, an order number and which item you bought). We never receive your card, bank or UPI details. Google's handling of payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Permissions

The game uses internet access to load ads and to verify purchases. It may also declare Android's advertising ID permission, which allows the ad SDK to read the advertising ID described above. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. How long information is kept

Game progress is kept on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as we need it to answer you, and then delete it. Information processed by Google for ads and payments is kept according to Google's policies.

9. Your rights

You can delete all game data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings › Apps › Bharat Rush › Storage › Clear data) or by uninstalling the game. You can manage ad personalisation as described in section 4. Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to ask about, correct or erase personal data about you, and to raise a grievance. To do so, email urjainfotech3@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

10. Security

We keep the data we handle to a minimum. Ad and payment data is sent through Google's services over encrypted connections.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, for example by adding analytics, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the game.
