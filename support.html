How to play

Controls

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. Signs with a red SLIDE tag must be slid under. Ticket checkers and dabbawalas can only be dodged sideways, not jumped.

Roof runs and bonus coins

Jump onto jeeps, autos and buses and run along their roofs. Each roof run, near miss and long coin chain adds to your score. Green ₹ notes are worth 5 coins.

Head Start and power-ups

Tap HEAD START during the countdown to begin with a boost. Magnets pull in coins and shields save you from one crash. Upgrade them in the Shop to make them last longer.

Common questions

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your phone, not online. Clearing the app's storage, using a "cleaner" app or reinstalling the game resets it. We can't restore it from our side.

The game feels slow or jerky

Open Settings in the game and choose a lower graphics quality. Closing other apps also helps. If it is still slow, email us your phone model.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep the game free. Most ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a reward such as a second chance or double coins.

A purchase didn't arrive

Make sure you are signed in to the same Google account you used to buy, then restart the game. If it still doesn't appear, email us your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.).

Delete your data

The game has no accounts, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To delete everything the game stored on your phone, go to Settings › Apps › Bharat Rush › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the game. To reset your advertising ID, see section 4 of the privacy policy. If you emailed us and want your messages deleted, just ask.
